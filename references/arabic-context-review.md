# Arabic contextual narration review

Review the spoken text by meaning before synthesizing. For each potentially ambiguous word, identify its referent and role in the sentence, then choose the vowel pattern and grammatical ending. Check connected speech and intended stops; do not add tanween to definite words or treat pausal endings as the only reading.

Channel-specific examples:

- **جُمَل**: sentences in a text; **جَمَل**: a camel. **جُمْلَة** is a single sentence. **تُجَمِّل** is a verb meaning to beautify. Never replace them from consonants alone.
- **الصِّيَاغَةُ الْأُولَى**: definite feminine noun/adjective agreement; no tanween. A wrong audio ending may occur despite correct spelling. Connected wording **هَذِهِ هِيَ الصِّيَاغَةُ الْأُولَى لِلْعِبَارَةِ** was used in the packaged comparison sample.
- **مُعَامِلُ الْعَدَدِ** means a coefficient; **مَعَامِل** can mean laboratories. **تَدْرُسُ** (you study), **تُدَرِّسُ** (you teach), **دَرَسَ** (studied), and passive forms require different readings.
- Read years and quantities with their nouns and syntax. For example **عَامَ أَلْفَيْنِ وَسِتَّةٍ** versus **سَنَةَ أَلْفَيْنِ وَسِتٍّ**. Do not change the historical year while fixing agreement.

For difficult passages, keep a compact review record: exact phrase, intended meaning, grammatical reason, expected reading, any spoken rephrase, source/spoken text hashes, and actual audio verification state. A root search or diacritics check finds candidates; it does not certify their meaning or pronunciation.

If correct diacritics are ignored by synthesis, add natural disambiguating context or use a meaning-preserving spoken rephrase, such as **جُمَلُ النَّصِّ** or **عِبَارَاتُ النَّصِّ** when the intended meaning permits it. Keep displayed wording separate, document any adaptation, and audition the complete sentence. Never claim the replacement guarantees correctness. Listen especially to homographs, number phrases, proper names, joins, and sentence endings.

The profile records the configured Hamed reference sample. Preserve that setting unless the channel owner requests a change. Generated episode audio still needs review; a correct transcript, healthy MP3, successful decode or approved audition does not validate every new utterance.

When a script changes after recording, preserve the old media and mark it as matching the previous script. Update text hashes and receipts; regenerate affected speech and recalculate scene timing before final delivery. Do not erase historical technical checks or present the old export as the corrected version.
